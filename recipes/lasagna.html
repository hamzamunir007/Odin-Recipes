Lasagna

Description

A hearty baked pasta dish with layers of noodles, meat sauce and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Brown the beef and mix with marinara sauce.
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes.
